01 The Basics
The Hockey Lab is a structured, recurring hockey development program built on a four-pillar system: Movement, Tracking, Execution, and Mindset. It's not drop-in skates or a one-off skills camp — it's systematic, ongoing development where every session connects to a larger plan and every athlete's progress is tracked over time.
Inside Express Performance Centre — 1020 Austin Ave, Unit 211, Coquitlam, BC.

02 The Training System
Most camps and skills sessions give athletes extra reps with no continuity — each session is stand-alone. The Hockey Lab is a system. Every session builds on the last, progress is tracked across four specific pillars, and athletes develop across their entire game. It's designed to produce measurable, compounding improvement over a season — not a one-time boost.
Every drill, training block, and coaching cue connects to one or more of these:

Movement — Skating mechanics, edge work, physical base, and mobility

Tracking — Visual awareness, reading the ice, and anticipation

Execution — Skill application under pressure, puck skills, and shooting

Mindset — Composure, reset routines, ownership, and confidence under pressure
Athletes don't just train — they get feedback. Progress across the four pillars is measured and logged, so coaching is responsive to where each athlete actually is, not where a generic program assumes they should be. Parents see real development, not just activity.
Technical sessions focus on skill mechanics — breaking down movement, edges, execution, and positioning in a structured, repetition-based environment. Compete sessions put those skills under pressure — game scenarios, small-area games, and competitive reps where athletes have to make decisions and perform. Both sessions are 45 minutes.
Mental Edge is The Hockey Lab's mental performance component — focus, composure, handling pressure, and building habits that transfer from the training environment to game situations. It's the Mindset pillar in action. Mental Edge is integrated into every Hockey Lab membership, and also available as a standalone 6-session program for athletes who want to focus on the mental side specifically.
The program is goalie-first — built by a goalie coach, with the synthetic ice surface and session structure optimized for goalie development. Skaters are welcome and the four-pillar system applies to every position. There is also a dedicated Goalie Development Camp (July 6–10) for athletes who want goalie-specific intensive work.
03 Who It's For
The program runs from age 5 through elite junior level, across four tiers. Athletes don't advance by age — they advance by demonstrated progress through the four pillars.
All levels. Every new athlete is assessed and placed in the right track at intake, regardless of age. A 10-year-old with strong fundamentals and a 10-year-old just starting out will be placed appropriately and coached accordingly.
A reasonable level of interest and willingness to work is all that's needed at the start. This isn't casual drop-in — it's structured development that builds on itself. Athletes who show up consistently get the most out of it.
