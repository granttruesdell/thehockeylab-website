The Story

BUILT FROM
THE GROUND UP

A year ago, Grant Truesdell built something in a backyard. No facility. No backing. Just a sheet of synthetic ice, a net, and a belief that the system he had in his head — built from 30+ years in the crease and a career performing under pressure on some of the biggest stages in music — could actually develop goalies differently.

"How often does your son train physically? Now compare that to how often he trains mentally. For a game that is largely mental, we spend far less time training the mind than the body."

That question — asked to a hockey parent in the first consultation — became the foundation of The Hockey Lab's four-pillar system: Technical. Physical. Mental. Tactical. Not a drill sheet. A curriculum. Not a camp. A system.

Now that system has a permanent home inside the Express Performance Centre in Coquitlam — one of BC's premier hockey training facilities. The Tri-Cities' first dedicated goalie development lab opens July 1, 2026. And we're inviting the community to be part of building it.
